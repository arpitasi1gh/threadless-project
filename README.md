## 🚀 Overview

**Threadless** is a modern single-page e-commerce platform that connects **independent artists** with **buyers** looking for unique, creative products.

It’s not just a store — it’s a **community-driven marketplace** where designs compete, trends evolve, and creativity thrives.

---

## 👥 User Roles

### 🛍️ Buyers
- Browse unique designs across categories  
- Add products to cart & wishlist  
- Follow artists & explore trending designs  
- Purchase items like t-shirts, hoodies, mugs, and more  

### 🎨 Artists (Sellers)
- Create and manage their own shop  
- Upload and showcase designs  
- Track engagement & visibility  
- Participate in design challenges  

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🧭 **Smart Navigation**
  - Explore by *Themes*, *Artists*, *Trending*, and *Categories*

- 🛒 **E-commerce Functionality**
  - Cart, Wishlist, Product Reviews  
  - Product variations (size, color, etc.)

- 🧑‍🎨 **Artist Dashboard**
  - Add/Edit/Delete products  
  - Manage shop listings  

- 🔥 **Community Engagement**
  - Vote on designs  
  - Submit designs for challenges  

- 🤖 **AI Chatbot Assistant**
  - Helps users discover products conversationally  

- 🎯 **Personalization**
  - Follow artists  
  - Discover curated content
